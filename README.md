# MSAIAtlas: bike-video roadmap

A watch-while-biking video plan for the UT Austin online MS in AI (Spring 2027) and for growing into designing large-scale GenAI tech stacks.

- Start with [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md): how to use it, the weekly rhythm, the order of play, how it maps to the UT courses, known gaps and what is pending.
- The three tracks are in [tracks/](tracks/): AI math (A), AI and ML concepts (B), the GenAI stack and enterprise architecture (C).

Every video was checked to exist (YouTube oEmbed returned the same ID, with title and channel copied as returned). None was watched, and most lengths are still unconfirmed. See "How much to trust this" in the roadmap.
